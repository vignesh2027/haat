How the game hides the chain

  1. When you entered, the game created your account and gave it two keys. The play key can only call the market contract. The recovery key has full control.
  2. Both keys sit in your browser in a sealed pass (an encrypted, tamper-proof cookie). The server keeps no database of keys. It opens your pass only while signing your move.
  3. Each move is a NEAR meta transaction: your play key signs it, and the village account relays it and pays the gas. That is why you never see a fee.
  4. If you clear your cookies, the pass is gone. Take the recovery key with you first if you want to keep the account.
